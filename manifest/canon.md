## Origin story

Both of us have spent the last five years building across different parts of crypto—stablecoins, lending, RWAs, wallets, NFTs and DeFi infrastructure.
The turning point came when we realized that even as full-time builders, it had become impossible to consistently keep up with everything happening across DeFi. Every week brought new protocols, better yields, new trading venues and different ways to deploy capital.
Helping friends enter DeFi was even more frustrating. What should have been a simple recommendation turned into explaining wallets, bridges, gas fees, multiple chains and half a dozen interfaces. Even after all that, there was no confidence they were accessing the best opportunity available.
We realized the problem wasn't that DeFi lacked useful financial products.The problem was that users were expected to become experts just to access them.Instead of building another protocol, we decided to build the layer that sits above them all.

Spectrum curates and aggregates the best opportunities across DeFi while abstracting away execution complexity so users can benefit from on-chain finance without needing to understand the underlying infrastructure.

## **Founder edge**

We have known each other for over a decade and have independently built products across crypto for more than five years.
Utkarsh leads Product and Growth, having previously worked on stablecoins, launchpads, NFTs and DeFi products.
Vishal leads Engineering with over ten years of software experience and has delivered more than fifty products across web, mobile, AI and blockchain. Between us we have experienced both sides of the market:

• Building DeFi products that struggle with distribution.

• Helping users navigate increasingly fragmented ecosystems.

Rather than building another financial primitive, we are building the aggregation and distribution layer that connects users to the best opportunities across the ecosystem.
