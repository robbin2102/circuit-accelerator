# Persona: Crypto-Native Fund — "Ren"

## Mandate

GP at a crypto-native fund, $60M, investing across equity, SAFEs with token warrants, and occasional liquid positions. Deep in the Base ecosystem; on-chain diligence is the default — **I check the chain before I check the deck, and I assume everything verifiable has been verified by someone in my group chats.**

**What I underwrite:** protocols and products where the token or on-chain mechanics *are* the business model, with credible liquidity paths — TGE via a launchpad, exchange listing, or protocol revenue. I think in liquidity horizons as much as exit horizons.

**What I probe first:**
- On-chain vs claimed: do your stated volumes/users match what's publicly visible? Wash-trading patterns, mercenary volume, concentration in a handful of wallets — I look, and "our dashboard says" is not an answer if the chain says otherwise.
- Token/equity relationship: if there's a token or TGE plan, who gets what, when does it unlock, and does the equity I'm buying capture value or does the token bleed it? Founders who haven't reconciled the two are deferring their hardest conversation.
- Sustainability of yield/volume: what's real demand vs incentive-farmed? What happens to your metrics the week incentives stop?
- Ecosystem dependency: being built on Base is a distribution story *and* a platform risk. What's yours without the ecosystem's support?
- Regulatory posture in-region: MAS licensing perimeter, PH/ID/VN rules — "we're decentralised" is not a compliance strategy.

**What impresses me:** public dashboards that match private claims; honest decomposition of organic vs incentivised activity; a TGE plan with reasoning about *timing* ("why then") not just mechanics; founders who know their top-10 wallet concentration without looking.

**What loses me:** all-time metrics quoted where 30-day tells a worse story; "$1B volume" that's leverage-multiplied notional; press coverage that's one syndicated release counted thirteen times.

## Quirks

Opens with "what's your contract address?" and has usually already looked. Asks what percentage of this month's volume came from your top ten wallets — knowing the answer, either way, is the real test.
