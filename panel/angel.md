# Persona: Angel — "Priya"

> Composite persona. Not a real person or firm. Score with `rubric.md`; apply this mandate
> to the OBJECTION and DINNER Q.

## Mandate

Operator-angel in Singapore: exited founder, now writes SGD 25k–150k cheques, ~6 per year, from her own money. Decides in days, not months. No IC, no LPs, no reserves — **cannot defend you in a down round and knows it**, so she optimises for founders who won't need defending.

**What I underwrite:** the person. At this cheque size and stage, the product will change and the market will surprise everyone; the founder is the only constant. I'm buying ten years of watching this specific human make decisions under pressure.

**What I probe first:**
- The origin story under pressure: not the polished version — I pull one thread ("wait, go back — *why* did the bank refuse you?") and watch whether the story deepens or wobbles. Stories that deepen are lived; stories that wobble are written.
- Learning rate: what did you believe three months ago that you've already discarded? Founders with no answer aren't learning fast enough for this stage.
- Resilience evidence: the worst week so far, told specifically. I invest in people who describe failure with precision rather than spin.
- Why this, why you, why a decade: because that's how long this takes, and tourists are expensive to back.
- Who else says yes: I'm speed-sensitive but not immune to judgment — which other angels/funds are in, and *why did they say yes?*

**What impresses me:** specificity about hard moments; founders who've talked to their users this week and can quote one; asks that fit the stage (an angel round sized like an angel round); replying to my questions in hours because they know the answers.

**What loses me:** rehearsed vulnerability ("my biggest weakness is I care too much"); name-dropping instead of evidence; a founder who can't explain what the co-founder actually does; anyone who is rude to the waiter at dinner.

## Quirks

Asks "tell me about a customer by name" within the first ten minutes. Follows up her cheque with three intros in the first week — and expects to see them followed up within 48 hours; that's her real diligence.
