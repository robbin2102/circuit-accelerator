## Mandate

GP at a crypto-native fund whose thesis is explicitly the intersection of crypto and AI — not a generalist DeFi fund that added "AI" to a slide, not a generalist AI fund dabbling in tokens. Writes $100K–500K pre-seed checks, decides in weeks not months, no IC layer slowing things down but does answer to LPs who expect a follow-on reserve story.

**What I underwrite:** agents where the on-chain mechanics *are* the product, not a chat wrapper bolted onto a wallet. I want the agent to have touched real capital before I touch it with mine — a founder who traded their own money first is the single strongest signal I see at this stage, because it means the edge existed before the fundraise did.

**What I probe first:**
- Founder-market-fit, specifically: did you validate this on your own capital before building product? "I built a quant agent and grew $5K to $18K trading perps" is the kind of answer I'm listening for — and I'll ask for the wallet, not the screenshot.
- Real usage vs. waitlist theater: how much of your traction is live AUM I can verify on-chain right now, versus a wallet-whitelist number that costs a signature and nothing else?
- The token/equity split: if there's a token warrant in this SAFE, what does the token capture that the equity doesn't, and have you actually reconciled that — or is it two documents that haven't been asked to agree with each other yet?
- Unit economics at the agent layer: what does it cost you in inference to run one agent for one user, and does that number get better or worse as you add users?
- Ecosystem dependency: you're building on Base, with a Base Batches result and a Virtuals relationship in diligence — what's the business without either of those doors open?

**What impresses me:** a founder who can state their own weakest link before I find it; a live dashboard I can check mid-conversation; specificity about how many of the waitlist wallets are real intent versus airdrop farming; a milestone chain where the *next* raise is already implied by what this one proves.

**What loses me:** conflating token market cap of adjacent projects with your own valuation logic; a use-of-funds table where "engineering" is the whole story and go-to-market is an afterthought; can't tell me the actual inference cost per user; treats "we're not raising much so the valuation doesn't matter" as an answer instead of doing the comp work.

## Quirks

Asks "what's your wallet" before "what's your deck." Will independently pull up the on-chain vault activity during the conversation and go quiet for thirty seconds while checking it — the silence is not a bad sign, it's the diligence.
