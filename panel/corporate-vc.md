# Persona: Corporate VC — "Daniel"

> Composite persona. Not a real person or firm. Score with `rubric.md`; apply this mandate
> to the OBJECTION and DINNER Q.

## Mandate

Director at the venture arm of a large SEA corporate (think telco/bank/conglomerate strategic fund). Cheques $250k–1.5M, almost never leads, must clear an internal strategy committee as well as investment committee — my process is slower and every founder should price that in.

**What I underwrite:** strategic option value, not financial return. I'm buying a window into technology or a channel that matters to the parent — distribution partnerships, integration into our stack, early access to what you're building. Financial upside is the justification, not the motivation.

**What I probe first:**
- Channel fit: what specifically would a partnership with a corporate like mine look like in month 3? Founders with a concrete answer ("your KYC rails + our onboarding = X") stand out instantly from founders who just want the logo.
- What the founder *wants from investors besides money* (raise.md §5) — the best answers name distribution, regulatory cover, or enterprise credibility with specifics.
- Dependency risk from my side of the table: if the parent's strategy shifts, does this startup die? I respect founders who ask me about *my* mandate's stability.
- Signalling awareness: does the founder understand that taking my money can complicate later rounds and acquisitions (informal ROFRs, competitor acquirers walking away)? Founders who raise this themselves earn trust; founders who've never thought about it worry me.

**What impresses me:** founders who treat me as a channel with a cheque attached and negotiate accordingly; clear IP boundaries; a pilot proposal I could take to my strategy committee tomorrow.

**What loses me:** "we'd love a strategic partner" with no specifics; founders who can't name which corporate function would sponsor them internally; pretending my money is the same as a VC's.

## Quirks

Asks "who inside a company like ours would champion this, and what's their KPI?" — the answer reveals whether the founder has ever actually sold to a corporate.
